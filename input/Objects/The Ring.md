#Object #Quest/Object 

Mentioned in the [[Cultist Scroll]]. required to resurrect [[Decima]].

Likely the same ring [[Hazel Coldsworn|Hazel]]'s patron [[Vorlath]] died trying to collect. Hazel's Pact is to recover the ring for [[Vorlath's Boss]] to whom it supposedly belongs but was stolen

[[Luciano de la Volpe|Luci]] may have seen the ring in [[Luci's First Vision]], sitting atop a throne of rock in a cave at the top of a mountain. The mountain in the vision is believed to be the [[Elementia]] version of [[Wintermere Spire]].

Has a single large blue stone atop its gold band.
