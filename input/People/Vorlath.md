#Person

Age: unknown
Race: unknown
Status: In the Astral Plane

Hazel's Patron. A being that contacted [[Hazel Coldsworn|Hazel]] this entire life until Hazel died in the Tree Hideout and made a pact with Vorlath to recover [[The Ring]] for [[Vorlath's Boss]]. Vorlath was able to return Hazel to life, give him magic, and bestow a magic sword upon him. Vorlath can only be contacted by being in proximity to death or undeath, or by wearing [[The Crown of the Revenant]]. 

Vorlath claims his [[Vorlath's Boss|patron]] wishes to remain anonymous. although he is willing to talk about her to a limited extent. He himself was on a quest to recover the ring for his patron. He had the ring and was on his way from [[Stormtalon]] to [[Wintermere Spire]] when he died.

Vorlath claims to know of [{Decima}], saying that his patron wishes she remains dead.
