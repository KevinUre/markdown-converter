#Person #Villain

Some sort of Demoness that [[The Cult of Decima]] seeks to resurrect. [[The Orcs]] claim to have defeated her in the past but she laid waste to [[Elementia]] before her demise. [[Vorlath]] fears her and claims [[Vorlath's Boss|his boss]] would prefer she stay dead. It would seem that her resurrect relies on the cult obtaining three artifacts: the [[The Ring]], the [[Bracelet of Sorrow]], and the [[Scepter]].
